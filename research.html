Research perspective

Moving computation closer to data can reduce costly data movement and help improve latency, energy, and area efficiency. The research interests represented here span circuit-level memory design, in-memory operations, system-level integration, and edge-AI applications.

Descriptions summarize the research topics listed in the academic profile; performance outcomes are not generalized beyond the specific projects and papers.
