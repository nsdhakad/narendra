Biography

Dr. Narendra Singh Dhakad is a semiconductor engineer and researcher working at the intersection of semiconductor design, EDA tools, in-memory computing, and AI hardware. Since June 2025, he has been working as an EDA Tools Hardware Engineer at Intel Foundry in Bengaluru, India.

His previous research appointments include a postdoctoral position at the Chair of AI Processor Design, Technical University of Munich (TUM), Germany, from August 2024 to June 2025, and a Visiting Research Fellowship at the Nanoelectronics Research Laboratory, Purdue University, USA, where he worked with Distinguished Professor Kaushik Roy. During his Ph.D., he also worked as a Processor Architecture Research Intern at Intel Labs in Bengaluru. Dr. Dhakad earned his Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Technology Indore (IIT Indore), India, in November 2024. His doctoral thesis was titled SRAM Based In-Memory Computing Architectures for AI Hardware, under the supervision of Prof. Santosh Kumar Vishvakarma. He received his M.Tech. in Microelectronics and VLSI Design from Shri G. S. Institute of Technology and Science (SGSITS), Indore, in June 2017. His master's thesis focused on the design of a low-phase-noise, wide-tuning-range voltage-controlled oscillator based on injection locking. He completed his B.E. in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Indira Gandhi Government Engineering College (IGEC), Sagar, in June 2015.

Before moving into full-time research and industry, he held teaching appointments at SGSITS Indore and IGEC Sagar. He served as an Assistant Professor at SGSITS from June 2018 to November 2019, a Lecturer at IGEC from July 2017 to May 2018, and a Graduate Teaching Assistant at IIT Indore and SGSITS. His teaching experience includes VLSI Technology, VLSI Design Lab, Analog Electronics, Electronic Devices and Circuits, Microprocessor Systems, and related digital and mixed-signal design subjects.

Dr. Dhakad contributed to the academic community as Founding Co-Chair of the IEEE Circuits and Systems Society (CASS) Madhya Pradesh Section, Founding Chair of the IEEE CASS Student Chapter at IIT Indore, and Founding Head of Outreach Activity and Webmaster of the IEEE Nanotechnology Council Student Chapter at IIT Indore. He has also served as a reviewer for journals including IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems I: Regular Papers, IEEE Transactions on Very Large Scale Integration Systems, IEEE Embedded Systems Letters, Circuits, Systems, and Signal Processing, and Scientific Reports.

His research interests span In-Memory Computing for AI Hardware, Emerging Memory Technologies, Memory Centric AI Computing Systems, Cryogenic Memory Circuits for Quantum Computing, Neuromorphic Computing, Silicon Photonics, and PDK Development. Across these areas, a recurring focus is to improve energy-performance efficiency through new memory, circuit, and system architectures.

Research interests

01 / AI HARDWARE

In-Memory Computing for AI Hardware

SRAM- and emerging-memory-based compute architectures to reduce data movement and improve AI throughput and energy efficiency.

02 / DEVICES

Emerging Memory Technologies

Research into RRAM, FeFET, and other memory technologies for computation, storage, and hardware acceleration.

03 / SYSTEMS

Memory Centric AI Computing Systems

Co-design of memory arrays, digital compute engines, and SoC-level architectures for data-intensive workloads.

04 / QUANTUM

Cryogenic Memory Circuits for Quantum Computing

Memory and interface circuits designed for low-temperature operation and the stringent constraints of quantum systems.

05 / NEUROMORPHIC

Neuromorphic Computing

Hardware concepts inspired by biological computation, with emphasis on efficient stateful and event-driven processing.

06 / PHOTONICS

Silicon Photonics

Photonics-enabled interconnect and computing concepts for high-bandwidth, low-energy data movement.

07 / EDA

PDK Development

Process design kit development, EDA flows, device and layout enablement, and hardware design methodology.
