Biography

Dr. Narendra Singh Dhakad is a semiconductor engineer and researcher working at the intersection of semiconductor design, EDA tools, in-memory computing, and AI hardware. Since June 2025, he has been working as an EDA Tools Hardware Engineer at Intel Foundry in Bengaluru, India.

His previous research appointments include a postdoctoral position at the Chair of AI Processor Design, Technical University of Munich (TUM), Germany, from August 2024 to June 2025, and a Visiting Research Fellowship at the Nanoelectronics Research Laboratory, Purdue University, USA, where he worked with Distinguished Professor Kaushik Roy. During his Ph.D., he also worked as a Processor Architecture Research Intern at Intel Labs in Bengaluru.

Dr. Dhakad earned his Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Technology Indore (IIT Indore), India, in November 2024. His doctoral thesis was titled SRAM Based In-Memory Computing Architectures for AI Hardware, under the supervision of Prof. Santosh Kumar Vishvakarma. He received his M.Tech. in Microelectronics and VLSI Design from Shri G. S. Institute of Technology and Science (SGSITS), Indore, in June 2017. His master's thesis focused on the design of a low-phase-noise, wide-tuning-range voltage-controlled oscillator based on injection locking. He completed his B.E. in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Indira Gandhi Government Engineering College (IGEC), Sagar, in June 2015.

Before moving into full-time research and industry, he held teaching appointments at SGSITS Indore and IGEC Sagar. He served as an Assistant Professor at SGSITS from June 2018 to November 2019, a Lecturer at IGEC from July 2017 to May 2018, and a Graduate Teaching Assistant at IIT Indore and SGSITS. His teaching experience includes VLSI Technology, VLSI Design Lab, Analog Electronics, Electronic Devices and Circuits, Microprocessor Systems, and related digital and mixed-signal design subjects.

Dr. Dhakad contributed to the academic community as Founding Co-Chair of the IEEE Circuits and Systems Society (CASS) Madhya Pradesh Section, Founding Chair of the IEEE CASS Student Chapter at IIT Indore, and Founding Head of Outreach Activity and Webmaster of the IEEE Nanotechnology Council Student Chapter at IIT Indore. He has also served as a reviewer for journals including IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems I: Regular Papers, IEEE Transactions on Very Large Scale Integration Systems, IEEE Embedded Systems Letters, Circuits, Systems, and Signal Processing, and Scientific Reports.

His research interests include in-/near-memory computing, SRAM-based computing architectures, AI processor and SoC design, memristor and emerging memory technologies, and energy-efficient hardware for edge AI. A recurring focus of his work is to reduce data movement and improve energy-performance efficiency by bringing computation closer to stored information.
