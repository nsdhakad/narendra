Biography

Dr. Narendra Singh Dhakad is a semiconductor engineer and researcher whose interests include in-memory computing for AI hardware and emerging memory architectures. He currently works as an EDA Tools Hardware Engineer at Intel Foundry in Bengaluru, India.

He received his B.E. in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Indira Gandhi Government Engineering College, Sagar; M.Tech. in Microelectronics and VLSI Design from Shri G. S. Institute of Technology and Science, Indore; and Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Technology Indore. His research and professional journey includes IIT Indore, Purdue University, Intel Labs, and the Technical University of Munich.

His work connects circuit design, memory architectures, and computing systems, with a focus on making AI hardware more efficient.

Research interests

01 / MEMORY

In-/near-memory computing

SRAM-based architectures, configurable multi-port memories, and digital/analog computing near data.

02 / SYSTEMS

AI SoC integration

Integration of RISC-V-based processors with in-memory computing engines and emerging memories.

03 / EDGE

Emerging memory & edge AI

Efficient hardware and algorithms for resource-constrained AI at the edge.
