01 ABOUT

Connecting the dots between
memory, logic & intelligence.

I am a semiconductor engineer and researcher interested in efficient computing architectures for AI hardware. My work explores in-/near-memory computing, SRAM-based architectures, SoC integration, and emerging memory technologies.

My path has moved between research labs and industry — from IIT Indore and Purdue University to the Technical University of Munich, Intel Labs, and Intel Foundry.
