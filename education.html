Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.)

Indian Institute of Technology Indore, India

Thesis title: SRAM Based In-Memory Computing Architectures for AI Hardware

Supervisor: Prof. Santosh Kumar Vishvakarma

My PhD thesis was focused on the complete framework of in-memory computing. I have explored circuit, architecture, and algorithm-level optimization to improve the AI hardware's latency, throughput, and area efficiency. The thesis includes SRAM architecture for content addressable memory and binary neural networks. CNN model has been modified for hierarchical classification and adaptability on new classes. We also proposed configurable multi-port memory architecture using single port 6T SRAM memory.
JUN 2017

Master of Technology (M.Tech.)

Shri G. S. Institute of Technology and Science (SGSITS) · Indore, India · Microelectronics and VLSI Design

Thesis title: Design of Low Phase Noise and Wide Tuning Range Voltage Controlled Oscillator Based on Injection Locking

Supervisor: Dr. R. C. Gurjar

This thesis proposed the designs and comparative analysis of architectures of Voltage Controlled Oscillators (VCO). VCO architectures have been studied in terms of their performance characteristics i.e. phase noise, tuning range, voltage swing and power dissipation. This thesis concerns the designs of VCO using the injection locking technique. Injection locking, synchronizes the frequency and phase of a free-running oscillator with a source. In this work, we use injection locking for ring oscillators and LC oscillators.
JUN 2015

Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.)

Indira Gandhi Government Engineering College (IGEC) · Sagar, India · Electronics & Communication Engineering

Project title: DTMF Based Traffic Light Control System

Supervisor: Mr. M. R. Ahirwar

The project included VHDL coding to control the traffic light. The aim of the project was to reduce the wait time when there is no traffic on a particular direction and also control the traffic from operator's room through mobile phone.
